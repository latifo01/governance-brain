# European AI Office (EAIO)

## Summary

The European AI Office is an organ within the European Commission responsible for the centralized enforcement of obligations for general-purpose AI models. The corpus documents it as defining systemic-risk thresholds and overseeing provider compliance for GPAI models, encouraging and facilitating GPAI codes of practice (AI Act Article 56), developing the fundamental rights impact assessment questionnaire template (Article 27(5)), and acting as market surveillance authority for AI systems built on GPAI models of the same provider (Article 75(1)). Providers of prohibited-practice-related AI systems have notification duties toward the AI Office.

## Applicability

This note applies to providers and deployers of GPAI models and GPAI-based systems: the AI Office is their counterpart for model-layer obligations, distinct from national market surveillance authorities for system-layer obligations.

## Governance Considerations

- Distinguish model-layer from system-layer supervision: GPAI providers answer to the AI Office; high-risk system providers and deployers answer to national authorities (see [[ai-system]], [[gpai-model]]).
- Track AI Office outputs that structure compliance: codes of practice, the FRIA template, systemic-risk designation decisions.
- Incident and non-compliance reporting routes for GPAI models go to the AI Office, not to national DPAs (unless data protection law applies separately).

## Limits

The establishment provision (AI Act Article 17) is not extractable from the local corpus (the French OJ text contains no "Office de l'IA" occurrences); the legal basis here rests on secondary sources' citations. The AI Office's internal procedures and guidance are not yet in the corpus and should be routed through the AI Act Knowledge Bank.
