# Societal impact and stakeholder feedback

## Purpose

This question checks whether impact evidence from the real context of use can
influence lifecycle decisions, rather than remaining outside the technical and
governance process.

## Guidance

Answer “yes” only when the project identifies affected groups and relevant
environmental concerns, defines an appropriate feedback or engagement route,
records how input is evaluated and shows an owner and decision path for
mitigation, escalation, appeal, redesign or decommissioning. The process should
be proportionate to the system's purpose and impact; a generic survey or a
one-time consultation is not enough when risks are ongoing.
