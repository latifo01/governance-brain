# Sustainability and societal impact metrics

## Purpose

Check that environmental and societal impact evidence can influence lifecycle decisions.

## Guidance

Answer "yes" only when the project identifies the relevant boundary and affected groups, documents a baseline and data limitations, selects proportionate indicators, assigns an owner and review cadence, and records how material findings change design, deployment, monitoring or retirement decisions.
