# Business purpose and intended outcome

## Purpose

Establish the use context against which scope, risk, testing, monitoring and
classification can be assessed. The intended purpose should be distinguished
from the system's internal objectives and from the way a deployer actually uses
it.

## Guidance

Describe the problem, expected users, intended outcome, decisions or workflows
influenced, data and instructions used, assumptions, foreseeable impacts,
prohibited or excluded uses, and the boundary of the approved use. Record the
system context and requirements in a form that can be revisited when the
deployment, users or operating environment changes.

## Governance considerations

Use the documented purpose to set the system boundary, identify relevant actors,
set risk tolerance and select proportionate testing, oversight and monitoring.
Purpose and context should remain linked to the accountable owner and to the
decision about whether the system may be used for the described workflow.

## Limits

The Commission material used here is non-binding guidance and the NIST AI RMF
is voluntary. This note does not determine legal classification or establish a
universal risk tolerance. The AI-system determination guidance is specific to
the assessed architecture, functionality and context; the visually unresolved
Page 13 candidate is excluded from this release.
