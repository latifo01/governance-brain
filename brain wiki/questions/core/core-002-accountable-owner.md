# Accountable owner

## Purpose

Identify the decision owner who can accept, restrict, stop or escalate use of
the system, and make the responsibility visible across its lifecycle.

## Guidance

Provide a named role or function, decision authority, lifecycle scope,
escalation route and any separate owners for model, data, product, deployment
or operations. Record how the owner receives information needed to make risk
decisions and how responsibilities are differentiated across human-AI
configurations.

## Governance considerations

Define and document roles, communication routes, competence expectations and
the decisions reserved for senior leadership or a governing body. Link the
accountable owner to the approved purpose, risk tolerance, oversight,
monitoring, incident handling and change decisions. Accountability should be
operational and reviewable rather than inferred from an organizational title.

## Limits

The evidence in this release consists of a limited ISO/IEC 42001 preview and
the voluntary NIST AI RMF. It supports governance context and expectations;
it does not create a legal obligation, certification conclusion or universal
organizational model.
