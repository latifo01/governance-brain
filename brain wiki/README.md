# Governance 360 Brain

This portable Obsidian vault organises governance knowledge and helps an assistant determine what to investigate and where to verify it. Official documentary evidence remains in the Governance 360 Knowledge Banks. Interpretations in this vault are not official regulatory evidence.

## Organisation

The CDO categories are AI concepts, AI Risks, AI Risk Mitigations, Data & AI Laws (including Data Privacy), Famous AI incidents, Norms & Frameworks, and Organizations. Questions have their own folder. Categories are navigation aids; frontmatter domains are authoritative.

## Maintaining the Brain

Use [[brain-schema]] for metadata. Write one focused concept or one bilingual question per file. Choose a stable kebab-case identifier, preserve it when changing a title, and add meaningful direct wikilinks to existing identifiers. Record precise document/page references in Source references. Do not copy whole documents or enterprise datasets.

Initial domains are AI, LEGAL, DATA_PROTECTION, INTERNAL_REGULATION, RISK, PROCESS and GENERAL. Additional domains are registered in the external workshop without changing the parser.

Knowledge Bank routing aliases are AI_ACT, AI_LEGAL_GUIDANCE, AI_NICE_TO_KNOW, AI_REGULATION_INTERNAL and DATA_AI_CLASSIFICATION. An empty list is valid where routing is unknown. Routing is not evidence verification.

The CDO material is currently being reviewed in the external workshop. Only reviewed content is integrated here; the workshop report describes actual coverage.
