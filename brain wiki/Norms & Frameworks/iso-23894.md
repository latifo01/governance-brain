# ISO/IEC 23894 AI risk management

## Summary

ISO/IEC 23894:2023 is guidance on managing risks specific to artificial intelligence. It is intended to be used in conjunction with ISO 31000:2018, whose article structure it mirrors — principles, organizational framework, and risk management process — with AI-specific extensions. Its scope addresses organizations that develop, produce, deploy or use AI products, systems or services, guiding them in integrating AI-specific risk into their existing activities and enterprise risk management.

The guidance restates the ISO 31000 risk management principles (integrated, structured and comprehensive, customized, inclusive, dynamic, based on best available information) with AI-specific implications, and works through the ISO 31000 framework clauses as applied to AI.

## Applicability

This note applies to any organization designing an AI risk management process aligned with recognized standards. It is guidance, not a certifiable requirements standard: it supports implementation of [[iso-31000]] for the AI context, complementing the management-system requirements of [[iso-42001]] and the functional taxonomy of [[nist-ai-rmf]].

## Governance Considerations

- Use it to extend an existing ISO 31000-aligned enterprise risk process to AI-specific risks rather than building a parallel process.
- Anchor AI risk in the organization's principles and framework layers (governance, roles, integration) before the process layer (assessment, treatment, monitoring).
- It complements supervisory model risk guidance ([[sr-11-7]]) where AI models fall under regulated model risk.

## Limits

The local corpus contains a preview edition of the standard: introduction, scope and principle tables are evidenced; the full risk process clauses are only partially covered. Nothing here certifies conformity of any organization.

## Related Concepts

- [[iso-31000]] is the parent risk management guidance that ISO/IEC 23894 extends.
- [[iso-42001]] turns risk treatment into management-system requirements.
- [[nist-ai-rmf]] offers a function-based alternative taxonomy (Govern/Map/Measure/Manage).
