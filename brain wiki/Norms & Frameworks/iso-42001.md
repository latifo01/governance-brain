# ISO/IEC 42001 AI management system

## Summary

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 specifies requirements and guidance for establishing,
implementing, maintaining and continually improving an AI management system
(AIMS). The preview presents adoption as a strategic organizational decision,
integrated with existing structures and risk-based processes. It describes an
AIMS as relevant to organizations providing or using products or services that
utilize AI systems.

## Applicability

Use this note as a management-system reference for AI governance, including
organizational context, interested parties, intended purpose, roles, risk and
opportunity management, controls and continual improvement. It can help relate
AI governance work to existing management structures and to the system's
documented scope.

## Governance considerations

- Identify the governing body, top management, accountable owner and relevant
  interested parties for the AI management system.
- Document the system boundary, intended results, roles and applicable
  requirements, then connect them to risk treatment and control evidence.
- Treat accountability, risk tolerance, documented decisions and continual
  improvement as linked management activities; [[nist-ai-rmf]] can provide a
  complementary risk-function taxonomy.
- Use [[core-001-business-purpose]] and [[core-002-accountable-owner]] to keep
  purpose and decision responsibility explicit.

## Limits

The local corpus contains only a limited preview: introduction, scope and
early definitions and clauses are evidenced. Full clause-by-clause coverage,
conformity assessment and certification conclusions require the complete
standard. This note does not claim certification or impose a universal legal
requirement. The source is a standard preview, not binding evidence.
