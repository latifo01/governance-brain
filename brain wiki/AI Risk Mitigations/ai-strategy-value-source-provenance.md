# AI strategy value — source provenance

## Summary

Strategy decisions are more reliable when each substantive assertion is traceable to a reviewed source unit, locator and evidence status.

## Applicability

This candidate is a governance coverage aid. It applies only when the organisation has an AI system, a defined purpose and a proportionate decision process; it does not collect project answers.

## Governance considerations

Keep source IDs, hashes, locators and review dates attached to claims. Separate binding law, guidance, frameworks and research when interpreting strategy options.

## Evidence to retain

Evidence lock, source/unit hashes, locator, review receipt and supersession record.

## Limits

Provenance proves traceability, not the truth of a business forecast or the applicability of a source to every project.
