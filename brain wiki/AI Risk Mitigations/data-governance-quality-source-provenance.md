# Data governance and quality — source provenance

## Summary

Quality claims require lineage from source data and transformations to the evaluated system or decision.

## Applicability

Preserve source identifiers, hashes, locators, transformation versions and review status.

## Governance considerations

Separate technical lineage from legal authority and record uncertainty when a supplier or dataset cannot be inspected.

## Evidence to retain

Lineage manifest, source/unit hashes, transformation log, supplier evidence and review receipt.

## Limits

Provenance supports reproducibility but cannot prove completeness, representativeness or legal applicability alone.
