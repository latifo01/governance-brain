# Data governance and quality — questionnaire coverage

## Summary

Questionnaire coverage should establish data involvement, purpose, quality evaluation, provenance, personal-data status and conditional privacy controls.

## Applicability

Ask the common data-quality question before activating personal-data, sensitive-data, DPIA or transfer modules.

## Governance considerations

Use stable topics and acyclic dependencies so the personal-data trigger gates conditional questions without duplicating the same intent.

## Evidence to retain

Question registry, dependency graph, data-quality evidence request and review record.

## Limits

Questionnaires publish catalogue items only; they do not collect answers or calculate a quality score.
