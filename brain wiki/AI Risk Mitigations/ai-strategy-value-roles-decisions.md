# AI strategy value — roles and decision rights

## Summary

A strategy decision is governable when a named owner, decision rights and escalation route connect the value hypothesis to deployment and change decisions.

## Applicability

This candidate is a governance coverage aid. It applies only when the organisation has an AI system, a defined purpose and a proportionate decision process; it does not collect project answers.

## Governance considerations

Name the accountable owner and decision forum. Record who may approve, restrict, pause or retire the system and how conflicts are escalated.

## Evidence to retain

Owner register, decision-rights matrix, approval record and escalation log.

## Limits

Role evidence is organisational guidance and must be adapted to the operating model.
