# Data governance and quality — lifecycle controls

## Summary

Quality is maintained through collection, preparation, validation, release, change, monitoring, rollback and retirement controls.

## Applicability

Apply controls proportionately across training, validation, test, benchmark and production data.

## Governance considerations

Version datasets and transformations, record change triggers and reassess quality when purpose, data distribution, supplier or deployment context changes.

## Evidence to retain

Lineage graph, version history, quality gates, change assessment, rollback and retirement record.

## Limits

Lifecycle controls are governance expectations drawn from reviewed frameworks; implementation thresholds remain context-specific.
