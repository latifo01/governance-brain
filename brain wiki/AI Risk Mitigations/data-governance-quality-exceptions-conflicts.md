# Data governance and quality — exceptions and conflicts

## Summary

Exceptions arise when data access, quality, privacy, transfer, retention or sector requirements conflict with the intended use.

## Applicability

Record the applicable scope before deciding whether an exception is permissible. Privacy and enforcement context cannot be generalized to all data.

## Governance considerations

Document conflict, jurisdiction, applicability trigger, owner, compensating control, expiry and escalation.

## Evidence to retain

Exception register, transfer or retention decision, legal review route and compensating-control evidence.

## Limits

The GDPR context rows are adjacent evidence only; this note does not resolve national derogations or legal conflicts.
