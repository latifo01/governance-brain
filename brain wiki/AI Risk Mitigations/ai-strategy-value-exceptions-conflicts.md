# AI strategy value — exceptions and conflicts

## Summary

Exceptions and conflicts are part of the strategy record because they explain when the intended value, risk appetite, legal scope or operating assumptions do not align.

## Applicability

This candidate is a governance coverage aid. It applies only when the organisation has an AI system, a defined purpose and a proportionate decision process; it does not collect project answers.

## Governance considerations

Record the exception, owner, rationale, expiry or review trigger and compensating control. Escalate unresolved conflicts instead of silently treating them as accepted value.

## Evidence to retain

Exception register, conflict decision, expiry/review trigger and compensating-control record.

## Limits

This note provides a governance pattern; it does not resolve legal conflicts or grant an exception.
