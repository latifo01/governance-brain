# Data governance and quality — definition and scope

## Summary

Data governance quality connects data purpose, provenance, representativeness, fitness for use, validation and risk context. It is not a single universal accuracy score.

## Applicability

It applies when data or evaluation results influence development, deployment, monitoring, decisions or retirement. Personal-data controls are conditional and follow their own applicability tests.

## Governance considerations

Define the intended use, data categories, quality dimensions, known limitations and decision consequences. Distinguish non-personal data quality from GDPR governance.

## Evidence to retain

Data inventory, purpose statement, quality profile, lineage and known-limitations register.

## Limits

Model-risk and privacy sources are scoped references; they do not establish a universal obligation for every dataset.
