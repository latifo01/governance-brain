## Editorial assessment

The lot opens the CDO "AI concepts" category with seven foundational, evidence-backed concepts. It gives the assistant a CDO-level route for:

- classifying AI systems by AI Act output category (predictions, content, recommendations, decisions) instead of marketing labels;
- recognizing content generation as a distinct output category with modality-specific measurement duties;
- recognizing agentic architectures (tool invocation, task decomposition, autonomous execution) and their deployment-dependent regulatory profiles;
- anchoring the whole vault on the Article 3(1) AI system definition and the model/system regulatory split (GPAI vs AI system);
- grounding the technique taxonomy (machine learning vs logic-/knowledge-based) in the official definition guidelines;
- framing RAG and similarity search as a trust-boundary concern connecting to the already-integrated risk notes.

## Important judgement calls

- All CDO drafts carried popular/marketing framings ("Traditional AI", product name lists, "AI that answers vs AI that acts"). None of these were verifiable in the corpus; the notes are reframed on the AI Act functional definition, the Commission definition guidelines, and the OWASP frameworks, all verified page by page.
- `predictive-ai` keeps the CDO aliases (including "Machine Learning") for navigation lineage, but the body does not assert the equivalence. The distinct `machine-learning` note now carries the technique taxonomy, so the alias overlap is deliberate navigation, not duplication.
- `agentic-ai` cites a working paper (SRC-0015) for the characterization; it is treated as descriptive of EDPS/Commission positions, not as binding law.
- The four added concepts (`ai-system`, `gpai-model`, `machine-learning`, `retrieval-augmented-generation`) were added to this lot on operator instruction after the initial three-file review, closing referential gaps the existing risk notes presupposed.
- No question is proposed: an output-category inventory question would partially overlap `legal-001-ai-act-classification-record` from lot 003, which is still awaiting human review. Deferred to avoid duplicate intent; revisit after lot 003 is decided.
