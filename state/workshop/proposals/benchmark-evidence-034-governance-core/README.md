# benchmark-evidence-034-governance-core

The assignment selects 35 validated Markdown units: 7 from SRC-0006, 9 from SRC-0017 and 19 from SRC-0039. Every selected unit retains its source SHA-256, unit content SHA-256, file SHA-256 and page locator. Non-selected units are outside this bounded extraction scope.

`SRC-0006` is an ISO/IEC 42001 document preview. The extraction records the preview limitation and does not infer unseen clauses, certification, or complete conformity. `SRC-0017` is non-binding Commission guidance; its Page 13 remains excluded pending visual review. `SRC-0039` is the voluntary NIST AI RMF. No BINDING evidence, legal obligation, or prohibition is introduced.

The audit retained 32 `VERIFIED` candidates and excluded `be034-src-0017-ec-009` (`UNCERTAIN`, visual review required). The receipt is staged at the locked shared evidence-library destination, and the three future-vault updates have exact section bindings. No active vault, configuration, index, `sources/` or `ingest/` file was modified. The candidate remains pending human approval.
