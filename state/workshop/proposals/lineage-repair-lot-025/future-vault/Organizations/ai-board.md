# European Artificial Intelligence Board (EAIB)

## Summary

Article 65 of the AI Act establishes the European Artificial Intelligence Board (in the French OJ text, "Comité européen de l'intelligence artificielle"). The Board is composed of one representative per Member State; the European Data Protection Supervisor participates in its work; representatives are designated by their Member States for three-year terms; Member States ensure their representatives meet defined conditions, and the designated representatives adopt the Board's rules of procedure.

The Board is an EU-level governance body composed of Member State representatives.
The cited Article 65 text also describes the European AI Office's participation
in the Board's meetings without voting rights.

## Applicability

This note positions the Board in the AI Act's institutional map. It records the
Board's composition, the Member State designation of representatives and the
coordination functions described in Article 65. It does not determine which
authority is competent for a particular provider, deployer or model.

## Governance Considerations

- Distinguish the Board from the AI Office (see [[european-ai-office-eaio]]):
  Article 65 gives the Board its own composition and gives the AI Office a
  non-voting meeting role.
- The EDPS participates as an observer, connecting the Board to the data
  protection institutional family (see [[edps]], [[edpb]]).
- Record the designated Member State representative, the single contact role and
  the adopted rules of procedure when using this note to route governance work.

## Limits

The Board's establishment and composition are evidenced from the French OJ text
of the AI Act (SRC-0011, pages 296-297). Article 66 tasks, operational practice,
opinions and working methods are not covered by these two units and require a
separate review.

## Related Concepts

- [[european-ai-office-eaio]] is the GPAI enforcement counterpart within the Commission.
- [[edps]] participates in the Board; [[data-protection-authority-dpa]] are the national enforcement counterparts.
- [[eu-ai-act]] is the establishing framework.
