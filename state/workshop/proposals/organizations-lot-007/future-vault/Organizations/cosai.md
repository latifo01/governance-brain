# CoSAI (Coalition for Secure AI)

## Summary

CoSAI (Coalition for Secure AI) is an OASIS Open Project bringing together an open ecosystem of AI and security experts, dedicated to sharing best practices for secure AI deployment. Its scope covers the secure building, integration, deployment and operation of AI systems; it explicitly excludes ethics, fairness and privacy topics handled elsewhere.

Its governance includes a Project Governing Board and technical standing committee co-chairs from Anthropic and IBM, with contributors from Google, Dell, Intel, NVIDIA, PayPal and Thomson Reuters. The corpus documents two dedicated CoSAI workstreams: Supply Chain Security and AI Agents. The framework documents ingested in this repository (incident response, shared responsibility, agentic management, identity and access, MCP security, preparing defenders, supply chain, signing ML artifacts) are CoSAI outputs.

## Applicability

This note applies when weighing the authority of CoSAI material in the Brain: it is industry best practice from an open standards venue, not a regulator and not a legal source. CoSAI outputs support control expectations (see [[aiuc-1]], [[mitre-atlas]]).

## Governance Considerations

- Cite CoSAI frameworks as recognized industry practice for AI security controls; do not present them as binding obligations.
- Participate in or track workstream outputs (Supply Chain Security, AI Agents) for early access to control guidance.

## Limits

The corpus does not state CoSAI's founders or founding date; claims about founding are not asserted here. A passage in SRC-0009 about Microsoft co-founding an organization in 2021 refers to C2PA (content provenance), not CoSAI — do not conflate them.
