# European Data Protection Supervisor (EDPS)

## Summary

The European Data Protection Supervisor is the EU institution-level data protection authority. Under the GDPR it provides the EDPB's secretariat (Article 75) and participates in the Board with voting rights on matters concerning Union institutions, agencies and bodies. The GDPR recitals record its consultation during the legislative process.

Under the AI Act, the corpus documents the EDPS as the designated market surveillance authority for AI systems used by EU institutions, agencies and bodies, including enforcement of the Article 5 prohibitions on prohibited AI practices. The EDPS has also characterized agentic AI in its TechSonar analysis (systems acting autonomously with limited human interaction to fulfil goals).

## Applicability

This note applies to EU institutions and bodies processing personal data or deploying AI systems: their supervisory counterpart is the EDPS rather than a national DPA. For private-sector deployments, the relevant authority remains the national supervisory authority (see [[data-protection-authority-dpa]]).

## Governance Considerations

- EU-institution AI deployments route supervision, complaints and breach reporting to the EDPS.
- The EDPS's interpretive outputs on AI (TechSonar, opinions) signal supervisory expectations for institution-level processing.
- Keep EDPS (institution-level) distinct from EDPB (Board) in compliance mapping; the acronym similarity is a documented source of confusion.

## Limits

This note reports the EDPS's documented roles; its enforcement practice for AI systems is still emerging in the corpus. Institution-specific procedures (staff regulations, EUDPR) are not locally evidenced in depth.
