## Editorial assessment

The lot strengthens the Brain on the legal and process side of AI governance. It gives the assistant a practical CDO-level route for:

- classifying AI systems under the AI Act;
- determining when a FRIA may be required;
- separating FRIA from DPIA;
- assessing automated decision-making safeguards under GDPR Article 22.

## Important judgement calls

- The EU AI Act note does not reproduce the prior CDO draft’s Digital Omnibus timeline. Those statements require a separate legal-update lot.
- ADMA is framed as an operational assessment artifact, not as a formal statutory instrument.
- `SRC-0022` is treated as training and explanatory support, not as the source of binding obligations.
- FRIA methodology is kept conservative. The academic FRIA article is noted as useful background but not used to create broad methodological requirements in this lot.
