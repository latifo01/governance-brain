# AI concepts P0 — evidence and scope limits

This candidate introduces six stable AI concept IDs from reviewed local
evidence. It uses fourteen validated Markdown units from `SRC-0039` (NIST AI
RMF 1.0) and `SRC-0036` (SR 26-2), with the prior hash-bound fidelity review
reused unchanged. The candidate contains no binding source, legal
interpretation, obligation, prohibition or certification claim.

The concepts are deliberately connected to existing notes rather than
replacing them. `ai-model-validation` and `ai-model-monitoring` remain the
operational notes; the new concepts provide shared terminology for lifecycle,
TEVV, explainability, robustness, adversarial ML and drift.

The evidence is bounded. NIST AI RMF is a voluntary framework. SR 26-2 is
supervisory guidance with a defined banking scope and is not a universal AI
governance rule. The evidence does not establish completeness, legal currency,
project-specific applicability or performance in a particular deployment.
Drift, robustness and adversarial behaviour remain context-dependent and can
include unobserved or emergent effects that the cited metrics do not capture.

The receipt excludes uncertain or visually unresolved candidates from the
source set. Future updates should add evidence only through a new reviewed
receipt and should preserve the six IDs unless a separate taxonomy decision
approves an alias or merge.
