# EU AI Act scope

## Purpose

Distinguish a confirmed legal route from voluntary use of the AI Act as a
governance benchmark.

## Guidance

Document relevant provider, deployer, importer or distributor roles, locations,
market or use in the Union, output use, exclusions, legal reviewer and the
version and effective date assessed.
