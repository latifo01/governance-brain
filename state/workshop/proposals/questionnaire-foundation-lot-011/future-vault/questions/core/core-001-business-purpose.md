# Business purpose and intended outcome

## Purpose

Establish the use context against which scope, risk, testing, monitoring and
legal classification can be assessed.

## Guidance

Describe the problem, expected users and outcome, decisions or workflows
influenced, prohibited or excluded uses, and the boundary of the approved use.
