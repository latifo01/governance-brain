# Automated decision-making trigger

## Purpose

Route relevant personal-data uses to Article 22 analysis and meaningful human
intervention safeguards.

## Guidance

Document the whole decision process, profiling, actual human discretion, legal
or similarly significant effects, controller, basis, exceptions and uncertainty.
