# Role-specific AI literacy and training

## Purpose

Check that people with governance, engineering, operational or oversight roles
can act within the authority and responsibilities assigned to them.

## Guidance

Answer "yes" only when the organisation identifies relevant roles, records the
training or competence expectations for each role, provides access to suitable
learning and reviews whether the content remains appropriate when the system or
role changes. This question records a governance control, not a legal
conclusion.
