# European Artificial Intelligence Board (EAIB)

## Summary

Article 65 of the AI Act establishes the European Artificial Intelligence Board (in the French OJ text, "Comité européen de l'intelligence artificielle"). The Board is composed of one representative per Member State; the European Data Protection Supervisor participates in its work; representatives are designated by their Member States for three-year terms; Member States ensure their representatives meet defined conditions, and the designated representatives adopt the Board's rules of procedure.

The Board is the Member State coordination body of the AI Act: it interfaces with the European AI Office's model-layer enforcement from the side of national authorities.

## Applicability

This note positions the Board in the AI Act's institutional map: providers and deployers principally face national market surveillance authorities and the AI Office for GPAI models, with the Board coordinating Member State positions and consistency across the system.

## Governance Considerations

- Distinguish the Board (Member State coordination) from the AI Office (Commission organ enforcing GPAI obligations — see [[european-ai-office-eaio]]); obligations are not addressed to the Board.
- The EDPS's participation connects the Board to the data protection institutional family (see [[edps]], [[edpb]]).
- Track Board outputs (opinions, coordination positions) as signals of evolving supervisory expectations; its rules of procedure and composition define who speaks for each Member State.

## Limits

The Board's establishment is evidenced from the French OJ text of the AI Act (SRC-0011, pages 296-297); operational practice, opinions and working methods are not yet in the corpus and should be routed through the AI Act Knowledge Bank.
