# ISO/IEC 27001 information security management

## Summary

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 specifies requirements for an information security management system (ISMS). The locally evidenced excerpt centers on its risk clauses: clause 6.1.2 (information security risk assessment), 6.1.3 (information security risk treatment, including producing a Statement of Applicability) and clauses 8.2-8.3 (ongoing information security risk assessment and treatment at planned intervals or when significant changes occur).

The Statement of Applicability is the traceability artifact of the standard: it contains the necessary controls and justifications for their inclusion or exclusion. Risk treatment requires comparing selected controls against those of Annex A and setting the treatment options (retain, avoid, mitigate, share the risk).

## Applicability

This note anchors the Brain's information-security vocabulary: AI systems inherit the ISMS context of the organization — the security management of AI components should extend the ISMS rather than operate beside it.

## Governance Considerations

- Extend the ISMS risk assessment scope to AI assets (models, training data, agent tooling) rather than creating a separate risk process.
- Keep the Statement of Applicability current when AI systems introduce new control needs (model signing, RAG access control — see [[traceability]] and [[retrieval-augmented-generation]]).
- Clause 8.2 makes risk assessment a recurring activity: AI-related threats (poisoning, prompt injection) should enter the ongoing assessment cycle, not a one-time project.
- Map ISO/IEC 27001 controls to AI-specific frameworks (ISO/IEC 42001 for the AIMS, AIUC-1 for agents) instead of duplicating evidence.

## Limits

The local corpus contains a 26-page excerpt edition covering the risk management clauses; full ISMS requirements (leadership, support, operation, improvement clauses) are only partially evidenced and are not asserted here. Nothing in this note certifies any organization's conformity.
