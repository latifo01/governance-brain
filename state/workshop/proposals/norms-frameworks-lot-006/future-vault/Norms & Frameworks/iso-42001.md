# ISO/IEC 42001 AI management system

## Summary

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 specifies requirements and guidance for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an AI management system (AIMS). Adopting an AIMS is presented as a strategic decision for the organization; the system is intended to be integrated with existing management structures and processes, and applied through a risk-based approach. The standard is applicable to any organization providing or using AI products or services.

An organization conforming with its requirements can generate evidence of its responsibility and accountability for AI systems. The standard uses harmonized management system structure and defines supporting terms (management system, control, statement of applicability).

## Applicability

This note applies to organizations that need a certifiable-style management framework for AI governance: policy, roles, risk treatment, controls and continual improvement across the AI lifecycle. It is the management-system counterpart to the risk guidance of [[iso-23894]].

## Governance Considerations

- Treat the AIMS as an extension of existing management systems (quality, security), not a standalone AI silo.
- The risk-based approach ties AIMS scope to the organization's AI risk register; the statement of applicability documents which controls apply and why.
- Mapping AIMS controls to supervisory model risk requirements ([[sr-11-7]]) avoids duplicate evidence for regulated AI models.

## Limits

The local corpus contains a preview edition: introduction, scope and early definitions/clauses are evidenced. The phrases "Plan-Do-Check-Act" and "certifiable" do not appear in the corpus and are not asserted here; conformity evidence is stated, certification language is not. Full clause-by-clause coverage would require the complete standard.
