# AI strategy value — risks and impacts

## Summary

A credible value case identifies material risks, affected people and trade-offs before deployment. Impact analysis qualifies what value can be pursued safely and within scope.

## Applicability

This candidate is a governance coverage aid. It applies only when the organisation has an AI system, a defined purpose and a proportionate decision process; it does not collect project answers.

## Governance considerations

Connect the intended purpose to affected groups, foreseeable harms, risk appetite and impact-assessment decisions. Use FRIA or DPIA routes when their separate applicability tests are met.

## Evidence to retain

Risk register, affected-party analysis, impact assessment decision and mitigation rationale.

## Limits

Impact evidence does not prove safety, lawfulness or a positive business case by itself.
