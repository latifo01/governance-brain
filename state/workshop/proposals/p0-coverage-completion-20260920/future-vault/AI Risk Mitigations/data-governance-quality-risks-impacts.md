# Data governance and quality — risks and impacts

## Summary

Data quality risks include non-representativeness, missingness, drift, leakage, bias, privacy risk and uncertainty about affected people or decisions.

## Applicability

Assess the data and deployment context together, including affected groups and material consequences. Invoke DPIA analysis only when its personal-data and high-risk triggers are met.

## Governance considerations

Link quality risks to intended purpose, impact, mitigation, residual uncertainty and decision rights.

## Evidence to retain

Data-risk register, representativeness analysis, impact assessment, mitigation tests and residual-risk decision.

## Limits

No single quality measure proves fitness, fairness, safety or lawfulness.
