# AI strategy value — evidence artifacts

## Summary

The strategy case becomes auditable when its purpose, assumptions, decisions, ownership and monitoring evidence are retained together.

## Applicability

This candidate is a governance coverage aid. It applies only when the organisation has an AI system, a defined purpose and a proportionate decision process; it does not collect project answers.

## Governance considerations

Maintain a minimal evidence package that links the strategy decision to system documentation, evaluation results, risk decisions and post-deployment observations.

## Evidence to retain

Strategy record, system card, evaluation summary, decision log and monitoring snapshot.

## Limits

An artefact demonstrates a control design or decision record; it does not prove that the expected value was realised.
