# Data governance and quality — metrics, monitoring and incidents

## Summary

Monitoring should detect material changes in data relevance, distributions, quality signals, model assumptions and incident patterns.

## Applicability

Define metrics and review cadence before deployment and connect deviations to investigation, remediation and use decisions.

## Governance considerations

Track data drift, missingness, validity, coverage, representativeness and incident signals where relevant.

## Evidence to retain

Metric definitions, dashboards or snapshots, threshold decisions, incident reports and remediation evidence.

## Limits

Metrics are context-dependent; the cited materials do not create a universal threshold.
