# Data governance and quality — evidence artifacts

## Summary

Evidence should make data selection, lineage, quality checks, validation, limitations and decisions reproducible.

## Applicability

When personal data is processed, retain the applicable records, security and DPIA artefacts alongside the technical quality record.

## Governance considerations

Link data-quality evidence to the model or system version and the decision it supports. Record missing access to third-party data as a limitation with a compensating-control decision.

## Evidence to retain

Data catalogue, lineage, quality results, validation report, ROPA/DPIA where applicable, security record and approval decision.

## Limits

A record demonstrates traceability and review; it does not establish that data is intrinsically good or that processing is lawful.
