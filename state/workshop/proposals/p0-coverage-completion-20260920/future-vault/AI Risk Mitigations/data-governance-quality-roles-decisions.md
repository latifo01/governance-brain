# Data governance and quality — roles and decision rights

## Summary

Quality decisions require accountable ownership across data providers, validators, model owners, controllers or processors where personal data is involved, and the escalation forum.

## Applicability

Use the personal-data trigger before applying GDPR controller/processor or record-keeping controls.

## Governance considerations

Assign data owner, steward, validator and decision authority. Where applicable, document controller/processor roles and processor instructions, contracts and assistance routes.

## Evidence to retain

Role matrix, processing-role analysis, processor agreement, validation sign-off and escalation record.

## Limits

GDPR evidence is conditional on personal-data processing; it does not define ownership for non-personal datasets.
