# AI strategy value — metrics, monitoring and incidents

## Summary

Value monitoring compares intended outcomes and risk indicators with observed behaviour, including incidents and material deviations.

## Applicability

This candidate is a governance coverage aid. It applies only when the organisation has an AI system, a defined purpose and a proportionate decision process; it does not collect project answers.

## Governance considerations

Define outcome, quality, risk and incident indicators before deployment. Record review cadence, thresholds, exceptions, remediation and the resulting continue/restrict/retire decision.

## Evidence to retain

Metric definitions, monitoring results, incident records, remediation decisions and lessons learned.

## Limits

Metrics are context-dependent and cannot be treated as universal success thresholds.
