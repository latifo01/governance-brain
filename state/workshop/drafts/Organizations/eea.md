**EEA** stands for the **European Economic Area**.

It represents the geographic and legal boundary within which the GDPR applies automatically, creating a single market for the free movement of personal data without the need for additional international transfer safeguards.
### Members of the EEA

The EEA consists of 30 countries:
- **27 EU Member States** (e.g., France, Germany, Spain, Italy)
- **3 Non-EU EFTA States:** **Iceland**, **Liechtenstein**, and **Norway**

_(Note: Switzerland is part of EFTA, but is **not** in the EEA. The UK left the EU/EEA following Brexit.)_
