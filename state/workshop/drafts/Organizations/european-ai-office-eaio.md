# European AI Office (EAIO)

### What is the EAIO?
The **European AI Office (EAIO)** is a body established within the [[European Commission]] (under DG CNECT) that serves as the central hub for AI expertise, governance, and policy coordination across the European Union. It combines technical, legal, and policy experts to oversee the safe and ethical development of artificial intelligence.

### When was it created?
It was officially established in **February 2024** (following a formal [[European Commission]] decision in January 2024), anticipating the final approval and implementation of the [[eu-ai-act]].

### Why was it created?
The EAIO was created to:
* **Ensure Centralized Governance:** Prevent fragmented rules across EU member states by providing a single, unified oversight body.
* **Oversee Advanced Models:** Monitor rapidly advancing "[[general-purpose-ai-gpai|General-Purpose AI]]" ([[general-purpose-ai-gpai|GPAI]]) models (e.g., Large Language Models like GPT-4) that carry potential systemic risks.
* **Balance Safety and Innovation:** Foster an ecosystem for trustworthy AI research and innovation in Europe while establishing guardrails to protect fundamental rights and human safety.
* **Lead Globally:** Position Europe as an international reference point for ethical AI regulation and standards.

### What is its link with regulation?
The EAIO is the primary enforcement and execution engine for the landmark **[[eu-ai-act|EU AI Act]]**:
* **Direct Supervision:** While national authorities handle lower-risk applications, the EAIO holds direct responsibility for supervising and enforcing compliance for providers of **General-Purpose AI models**.
* **Codes of Practice:** It drafts guidelines, standard operating procedures, and codes of practice to help companies comply with the AI Act.
* **Investigation & Penalties:** It has the power to request information, conduct evaluations, and recommend sanctions or fines for non-compliant AI providers.
* **European AI Board Support:** It provides administrative and scientific support to the European AI Board, which consists of representatives from all EU Member States.
