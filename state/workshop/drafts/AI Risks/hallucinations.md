In the context of Large Language Models (LLMs), **hallucination** refers to a phenomenon where the model generates content that is incorrect, nonsensical, or entirely ungrounded in reality, while presenting it with confidence.

These fall into two main types:
- **Factual Hallucination:** Stating false information as fact (e.g., attributing a non-existent book to an author or inventing historical events).
- **Faithfulness Hallucination:** Misrepresenting or contradicting information provided directly in the prompt or reference context.
