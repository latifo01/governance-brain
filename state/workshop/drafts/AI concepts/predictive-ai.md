**Predictive AI** (also known as Traditional AI or Machine Learning) uses historical data to identify patterns and **forecast future outcomes, classify information, or make decisions** (e.g., predicting sales trends, detecting fraud, or recommending products).

**By Opposition to Generative AI:**
* **Predictive AI** *analyzes* data to give a **prediction or decision** (Output: A number, label, or probability).
* **Generative AI** *uses* data to **create brand-new content** (Output: New text, images, audio, or code).
