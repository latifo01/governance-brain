A **Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)** is a structured risk assessment process designed to identify and minimize privacy risks associated with data processing activities.

Under the GDPR (Article 35), organizations are legally required to conduct a DPIA before launching any new processing operation that is **likely to result in a high risk** to individuals' rights and freedoms—such as deploying automated profiling, monitoring public spaces at scale, or using new technologies like AI to process sensitive personal data.
