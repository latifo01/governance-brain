A **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** is an independent security and legal compliance expert designated by an organization to monitor GDPR compliance, advise on data protection obligations, conduct impact assessments (DPIAs), and act as the main point of contact for individuals and Data Protection Authorities.

Under the [[gdpr]], appointing a DPO is mandatory for public authorities, organizations performing large-scale systematic monitoring of individuals, or those processing special category (sensitive) data on a large scale.
