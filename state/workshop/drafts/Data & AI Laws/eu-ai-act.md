The **European Union Artificial Intelligence Act** (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) is a horizontal regulatory framework governing the development, deployment, and use of AI systems across the EU single market. It adopts a **risk-based approach**, imposing legal obligations proportionate to the potential level of harm an AI system poses to health, safety, and fundamental rights.
## Key Deployment Milestones

The AI Act follows a phased implementation plan. In July 2026, the EU adopted the **[[digital-omnibus]] on AI**, which modified specific compliance deadlines—most notably extending high-risk system timelines to allow organizations and market surveillance authorities sufficient preparation time—while expanding regulatory sandboxes and introducing targeted prohibitions.

| **Date**                               | **Deployment Milestone & Scope**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1 August 2024**                      | **Entry into Force** of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **2 February 2025**                    | **Initial Prohibitions & Definitions:** Enforceability of forbidden AI practices (Article 5) and initial AI literacy support provisions.                                                                                                                                          |
| **2 August 2025**                      | **GPAI & Governance:** Rules for [[general-purpose-ai-gpai]] models apply; EU governance structure ([[EU AI Office]], [[ai-board]]) and Member State competent authorities established.                                                                                         |
| **2 August 2026**                      | **General Transparency Rules:** Article 50 transparency requirements (informing users when interacting with AI, emotion recognition disclosure, deepfake labeling) start to apply.                                                                                                |
| **2 December 2026** _(Omnibus Update)_ | **Expanded Prohibitions & Synthetic Content Transition:** Enforcement of new Digital Omnibus prohibitions (e.g., AI tools generating non-consensual intimate deepfakes/CSAM) and transitional rules for legacy GPAI synthetic content tools under Article 50(2).                  |
| **2 August 2027**                      | **Regulatory Sandboxes:** Member States must have at least one operational national AI regulatory sandbox; legacy pre-August 2025 GPAI models must achieve full compliance.                                                                                                       |
| **2 December 2027** _(Omnibus Update)_ | **Annex III High-Risk AI Rules:** Compliance requirements and Fundamental Rights Impact Assessments (FRIA) take effect for standalone high-risk AI (biometrics, employment, essential public services, law enforcement, administration of justice). _[Deferred from August 2026]_ |
| **2 August 2028** _(Omnibus Update)_   | **Annex I Embedded High-Risk AI Rules:** Rules take effect for high-risk AI embedded as safety components in regulated physical products (machinery, medical devices, aviation, toys, elevators). _[Deferred from August 2027]_                                                   |

## Levels of Criticality (Risk Matrix)

| **Level of Criticality**                            | **Definition**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | **Key Obligations / Requirements**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Unacceptable Risk** _(Prohibited)_             | AI systems considered a clear threat to safety, livelihoods, and fundamental human rights. List of prohibited Uses :• **Cognitive behavioral manipulation** exploiting vulnerabilities (age, disability).<br>• **Social scoring** by governments or private entities.<br>• **Real-time remote biometric identification in publicly accessible spaces** for law enforcement (with strict, narrow exceptions).<br>• **Untargeted scraping of facial images** from the internet or CCTV.<br>• **Emotion recognition in workplaces** and educational institutions.<br>• **Biometric categorization predicting sensitive attributes** (race, political views, sexual orientation).<br>• **Predictive policing** based solely on profiling or personality traits.<br>• _Digital Omnibus addition:_ **AI tools generating non-consensual sexually explicit content** or CSAM. | **Total ban.** No placement on the market, put into service, or use permitted within the EU.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **2. High Risk**                                    | AI systems that pose significant risks to health, safety, fundamental rights, the environment, or rule of law. Split into Annex III (stand-alone software) and Annex I (safety components of regulated products).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | • **Risk management system** maintained throughout system lifecycle.<br>• **Data governance** standards (high-quality, non-biased training sets).<br>• **Detailed technical documentation** & **record-keeping** (logging).<br>• **Transparency** & **instruction provision** for deployers.<br>• **[[human-oversight]]** capabilities built into the system design.<br>• **High accuracy**, **cybersecurity**, and **robustness**.<br>• **[[fundamental-right-impact-assessment-fria]]** (FRIA) prior to deployment.<br>• Ex-ante conformity assessment and registration in the EU database. |
| **3. Medium / Specific Risk** _(Transparency Risk)_ | AI systems with specific transparency risks where individuals must be aware they are interacting with or consuming AI-generated content (e.g., chatbots, emotion recognition, deepfakes, text/image generators).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | • **Interaction Disclosure:** Clear notice informing humans when interacting with an AI system.<br>• **Synthetic Content Watermarking:** Technical marking/labeling of machine-generated text, audio, image, and video outputs in machine-readable formats.<br>• **Deepfake Labeling:** Explicit labeling of synthetic media (deepfakes) published to inform the public.<br>• **Text Disclosure:** Public-interest text generators must label outputs unless subjected to human editorial oversight.                                                                                     |
| **4. Minimal / Low Risk**                           | All other AI systems that present minimal or no risk to fundamental rights or safety (e.g., AI-enabled spam filters, video games).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | • **No compulsory obligations.**<br>• Encouraged voluntary adoption of voluntary codes of conduct.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
## Jurisdiction

EU AI Act applies to:
* All legal entities operating in [[eea]]
* All legal entities offering specific services to [[eea]] citizens
