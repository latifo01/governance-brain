**ISO/IEC 23894** (_Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Guidance on risk management_) is an international standard published in **February 2023** by the ISO and IEC. It provides actionable guidelines for managing risks specific to artificial intelligence (AI) across an organization.

### Core Purpose & Scope

The standard applies to any organization—whether developing, deploying, producing, or utilizing AI products, systems, or services. Rather than creating a standalone risk methodology, ISO/IEC 23894 **extends [[iso-31000]]** (the general risk management framework) specifically to address the unique challenges of AI.

### Key Concepts & Focus Areas

ISO/IEC 23894 adapts standard risk governance to cover AI-specific factors:

- **Data Integrity & Governance:** Addressing dataset bias, data privacy, minimization, and continuous quality control.
    
- **Dynamic & Autonomous Behavior:** Managing risks related to non-deterministic outputs, concept drift, and models that adapt over time.
    
- **Transparency & Explainability:** Evaluating black-box risks and accountability in automated decision-making.
    
- **Broader Societal Impact:** Extending beyond traditional operational risks to assess ethical considerations, human rights impacts, environmental footprints, and community effects.
    

### Relation to Other AI Standards

| **Standard**                     | **Role & Focus**                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ISO/IEC 23894**                | **Risk Guidance:** Tells organizations _how to manage and mitigate risks_ specific to AI systems.                                                                                                                 |
| **[[ISO 42001\|ISO/IEC 42001]]** | **Management System (AIMS):** The overarching framework for establishing, implementing, and auditing an AI Management System in an organization. ISO 23894 feeds risk criteria directly into ISO 42001 processes. |
| **[[iso-31000]]**                | **Base Risk Standard:** General enterprise risk management guidelines that ISO 23894 mirrors and extends.                                                                                                         |
