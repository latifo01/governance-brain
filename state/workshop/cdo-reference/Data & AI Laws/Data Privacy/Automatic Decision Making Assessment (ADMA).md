In the context of the [[GDPR]], an **Automatic Decision Making Assessment (ADMA)** is a formal evaluation process—often conducted as part of a [[Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA)]]—used to identify, assess, and mitigate the risks associated with fully automated decisions (including profiling) that significantly affect individuals.

Under **GDPR Article 22**, individuals have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing if those decisions have legal or similarly significant impacts (e.g., automated loan denials or recruitment filtering). 

**In short, an ADMA ensures that an automated system is:**
* **Fair and Non-discriminatory:** Free from algorithmic bias.
* **Transparent:** Explainable to the individuals affected.
* **Compliant:** Equipping users with the right to request human intervention, challenge the decision, and express their point of view.
