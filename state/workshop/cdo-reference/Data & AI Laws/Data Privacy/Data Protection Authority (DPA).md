Data Protection Authorities (DPAs)—referred to in the GDPR as "Supervisory Authorities"—are independent public bodies responsible for monitoring and enforcing data protection laws across Europe.

The list of DPAs in Europe is structured across EU-level bodies, EU Member States, EEA countries, and non-EEA European nations.

### EU-Level Supervisory Bodies

- **European Data Protection Board (EDPB):** An independent EU body that ensures consistent application of the GDPR across the EU/EEA. It consists of the heads of all national DPAs and the EDPS.
- **European Data Protection Supervisor (EDPS):** Supervises personal data processing carried out by EU institutions, bodies, offices, and agencies.
