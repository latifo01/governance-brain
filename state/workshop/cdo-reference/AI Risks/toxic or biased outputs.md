In the context of Large Language Models (LLMs), **toxic or biased outputs** refer to generated text that reflects, amplifies, or creates harmful, discriminatory, abusive, or unfair content derived from patterns in its training data or flawed alignment tuning.
- **Toxic Outputs:** Content that contains hate speech, harassment, explicit profanity, threats of violence, or instructions for self-harm or illegal acts.
- **Biased Outputs:** Content that demonstrates systematic unfairness, cultural or demographic stereotypes, or unequal treatment based on protected characteristics like race, gender, religion, age, or socioeconomic status.
### Examples

1. **Occupational Gender Stereotyping (Bias):** Automatically completing prompts about roles with gendered assumptions—such as referring to a generic "doctor" as _he_ and a generic "nurse" or "secretary" as _she_.
2. **Hate Speech & Slurs (Toxicity):** Generating slurs, derogatory generalizations, or demeaning characterizations targeted at specific ethnic, religious, or minority groups when given adversarial or provocative prompts.
3. **Toxic Framing & Toxicity Amplification (Toxicity/Bias):** Adopting abusive tone or toxic arguments when summarizing controversial debate topics, or validating harmful premises introduced in user prompts.
