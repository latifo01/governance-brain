- The MIT AI Risk Initiative provides an evidence-based repository and taxonomy designed to help policymakers and practitioners identify, categorize, and manage artificial intelligence risks.
- The initiative offers tools to analyze expert consensus on severe AI harms, track real-world AI incidents, and map global governance frameworks against specific risk domains.
- Experts in AI safety and governance describe the project as a critical, empirical resource that facilitates the transition from theoretical frameworks to actionable, risk-based strategies.

Link to [MIT AI Risk Initiative](https://airisk.mit.edu/)
