**ISO/IEC 42001** is the first certifiable international standard designed specifically for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an **Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS)**.
Jointly published by the International Organization for Standardization ([[ISO 23894]]) and the International Electrotechnical Commission ([[IEC]]), it provides a structured framework for organizations that develop, supply, or deploy AI technologies.
# Core Structure
ISO 42001 adopts the **Harmonized Structure (Annex SL)** used by other major management system standards (such as ISO 27001 for Information Security and ISO 9001 for Quality Management). This allows organizations to integrate AI governance into their existing compliance infrastructure.
The standard consists of two main operational layers:
## 1. System Clauses (Clauses 4–10)
Defines the management process for governing AI within an enterprise:
- **Context (Clause 4):** Identifying organizational stakeholders, AI deployment boundaries, and internal/external risk factors.
- **Leadership (Clause 5):** Establishing clear accountability, executive oversight, and an overarching AI Policy.
- **Planning (Clause 6):** Conducting AI risk assessments and mapping strategic AI objectives.
- **Support & Operation (Clauses 7–8):** Managing resources, competency, operational controls, and execution throughout the AI system lifecycle.
- **Evaluation & Improvement (Clauses 9–10):** Executing internal audits, executive reviews, incident response, and continuous optimization.
## 2. Annex A Controls
Provides a reference set of AI-specific controls across key functional domains:
- **AI Policies & Governance:** Operational guidelines for AI development and deployment.
- **Resource & Data Management:** Data quality, provenance, lifecycle tracking, and computing infrastructure.
- **AI System Lifecycle:** Verification, validation, testing, deployment, and ongoing monitoring.
- **[[AI Impact Assessment (AIIA)]]:** Evaluating societal, legal, safety, and ethical impacts.
- **Third-Party Management:** Auditing external AI vendors, foundation model providers, and third-party tools.
