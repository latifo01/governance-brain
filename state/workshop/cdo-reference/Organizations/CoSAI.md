- The Coalition for Secure AI (CoSAI) is an open ecosystem composed of AI and security experts from industry-leading organizations.

- The primary mission of CoSAI is to share best practices for the secure deployment of AI and to collaborate on research and product development related to AI security.

- CoSAI operates under the governance of [OASIS Open](https://app.recall.it/item/a5395f8a-0788-4981-ae85-83fb6598f321), which is an international standards and open-source consortium.

- The coalition emphasizes that security requires collective action and advocates for the use of AI to secure AI systems.

- To ensure safe participation in the digital ecosystem, CoSAI promotes the adoption of common security standards and best practices among individuals, developers, and companies.

- CoSAI fosters a collaborative environment for diverse stakeholders to invest in research, share expertise, and develop technical open-source solutions and methodologies for AI development and deployment.

- Since its launch, the coalition has worked with industry and academic partners across several critical workstreams to strengthen AI security.

- Participants in CoSAI gain access to a network of industry leaders, standardized methodologies, and resources designed to help organizations implement robust security controls and mitigations.

- The coalition aims to set new standards for the integrity and safety of AI systems to protect organizations and maintain consumer trust.

- Technical participation in CoSAI is free and open to all developers, and the organization maintains a commitment to providing an open and welcoming environment for contributors.

- Interested parties can access code, documentation, and project information through the CoSAI [GitHub](https://app.recall.it/item/0ea0261c-e386-4c8c-8b9c-949333c050ba) repositories.

- CoSAI invites organizations to become sponsors to provide the necessary funding for community services and to ensure the project's success.
