## Definition
**Agentic AI** refers to advanced artificial intelligence systems designed with **autonomy, goal-orientation, and decision-making capabilities**. Unlike traditional AI that passively responds to specific human prompts, Agentic AI can independently break down complex objectives, plan multi-step workflows, execute actions using external tools, and adapt to changing circumstances with minimal human intervention.
## Short Description
Agentic AI marks a fundamental paradigm shift from **"AI that answers" to "AI that acts."** 
While standard generative AI operates like a smart assistant waiting for instructions, Agentic AI acts as an autonomous agent. Given a high-level goal (e.g., *"Analyze market trends and draft a product strategy"*), it reasons through the problem, decides which tools or APIs to use, executes tasks, evaluates its own progress, and self-corrects along the way to deliver a finished outcome. 

**Key Characteristics:**
* **Autonomy:** Operates independently to achieve complex outcomes.
* **Proactive Planning:** Breaks large goals into logical, sequential sub-tasks.
* **Tool Integration:** Interacts with web browsers, databases, APIs, and software.
* **Adaptability:** Uses feedback loops to correct errors and adjust strategies dynamically.
