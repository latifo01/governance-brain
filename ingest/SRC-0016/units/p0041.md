> 3.3.2. With the objective or the effect of materially distorting behaviour  
> (113) The third condition for the prohibition in Article 5(1) (b) AI Act to apply is that the 
> exploitation of the vulnerabilities examined above must have either a) ‘the objective’ 
> or b) ‘the effect of materially distorting the behaviour of a person or a group of persons’. 
> This implies a substantial impact , rather than a minor  or trivial one , but does not 
> necessarily require intent, since Article 5(1)(b) AI Act covers practices that may only 
> have the ‘effect’ of causing material distortion s. Article 5(1)(a) and (b)  AI Act make 
> use of the same concept s and should therefore be interpreted in the same way. The 
> explanations provided in section 3.2.2. are therefore equally relevant for Article 5(1)(b)
