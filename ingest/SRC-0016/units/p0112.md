> (357) First, the use of real-time RBI systems  in publicly accessible spaces for law 
> enforcement purposes  is only allowed  to ‘confirm the identity of the specifically 
> targeted individual.’ This first condition aims to balance the seriousness of the situation 
> and the harm resulting from not using the system with the impact of the technology on 
> individuals’ rights and freedoms. It aims to avoid mass surveillance by targeting an 
> individual for the deployment of real-time RBI. As a consequence, the deployment of 
> a real-time RBI system in publicly accessible spaces  for law enforcement purposes  
> should only be authorized for targeted individuals.
