> factors external to the AI system  and which are not within the control and reasonably

> 3.3.  Main components of the prohibition in Article 5(1)(b) AI Act – harmful 
> exploitation of vulnerabilities 
> Article 5(1)(b) AI Act provides: 
> 1. The following AI practices shall be prohibited: 
> (b) the placing on the market, the putting into service or the use of an AI system that 
> exploits any of the vulnerabilities of a natural person or a specific group of persons due 
> to their age, disability or a specific social or economic situation, with the objecti ve, or 
> the effect, of materially distorting the behaviour of that person or a person belonging to 
> that group in a manner that causes or is reasonably likely to cause that person or another 
> person significant harm; 
> (98) Several cumulative conditions must be fulfilled for the prohibition in Article 5(1)(b) AI 
> Act to apply: 
> (i) The practice must constitute the ‘placing on the market’, the ‘putting into service’, 
> or the ‘use’ of an AI system.
