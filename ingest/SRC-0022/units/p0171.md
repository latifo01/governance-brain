> Prompt for reflection 
> InnovaHospital’s deployment of AI diagnostic tools raises concerns about technical and 
> organizational measures. Consider how different contexts (e.g., medical settings vs. 
> educational institutions) require tailored approaches to risk management. How can 
> organizations like InnovaHospital ensure that measures address the unique risks posed 
> by their AI systems, and how might these approaches differ from those needed by 
> UNw? 
> Answer sheet 
> Exercise 1. Alternative E is correct. Alternatives A and D refer to measures that are 
> desirable from a business perspective but have limited use for data protection. 
> Alternatives B and D identify factors that are relevant for some aspects of data 
> protection, but not for others. For example, data subjects have rights even when they 
> are not particularly cost-effective. 
> Exercise 2. Alternative A is correct. Alternatives B and E adopt a reactive approach to 
> risk. Alternative C can be part of a broader risk management strategy, but it does not 
> offer an ongoing evaluation of risk. Finally, Alternative C might be desirable for other 
> reasons but does not offer extra insight about the risk landscape.  
> Exercise 3. Alternative B is correct. Alternative A would restrict utility without addressing 
> risks, alternative C might struggle with the difficulties of consent in AI contexts.
