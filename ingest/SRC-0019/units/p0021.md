> 3.3.2 Considerations on the three steps of the legitimate interest assessment in the context 
> of the development and deployment of AI models 
> 66. In order to determine whether a given processing of personal data may be based on Article 6(1)(f) 
> GDPR, SAs should verify that controlle rs have carefully assessed and documented whether the three 
> following cumulative conditions are met: (i) the pursuit of a legitimate interest by the controller or by 
> a third party; (ii) the processing is necessary to pursue the legitimate interest;  and (iii) the legitimate 
> interest is not overridden by the interests or fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subjects50.  
> 3.3.2.1 First step - Pursuit of a legitimate interest by the controller or by a third party 
> 67. An interest is the broader stake or benefit that a controller or third party may have in engaging in a 
> specific processing activity 51. While the GDPR and the CJEU recognised several interests as being 
> legitimate52, the assessment of the legitimacy of a given interest should be the result of a case-by-case 
> analysis.
