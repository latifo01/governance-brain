# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Governance Brain aims to provide a respectful, constructive environment for
everyone participating in the project, regardless of background, identity,
experience or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Be courteous and considerate in issues, pull requests and project discussions.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and distinguish facts from interpretation.
- Discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality, and acknowledge others' contributions.
- Accept corrections and help resolve disagreements constructively.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate
intimidation and disclosure of private information without consent are
unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to project collaboration spaces and to conduct when someone
represents the project in other spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner, **@latifo01**, using an
available private contact channel. Contact information is listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/latifo01) when provided.
Do not post personal information or sensitive incident details in a public
issue. If a private channel is unavailable, request one before sharing details.

The maintainer reviews reports with discretion, considers the affected people
and may request a correction, remove content, or restrict participation.
Responses should be proportionate to the behaviour and its impact. Participants
may ask the maintainer to reconsider a decision with additional context.

For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
