# Vault contract

Keep the existing category paths under `brain wiki/`. Allowed note types are
`knowledge`, `question`, and `index`. Questions remain under `questions/` and
new domain navigation belongs under `indexes/`. Do not mix official source
originals or workshop drafts into the active vault.

Use stable filenames derived from IDs when a display label may change. A link target must exist or be declared as a reviewed proposal; unresolved strings do not become placeholder notes automatically.

Index notes contain only useful navigation and no unsupported regulatory
conclusions. Markdown question notes are canonical; any Canvas view is derived.
Avoid timestamps in deterministic notes unless the timestamp is part of reviewed
source metadata or an approval event.
